Summary

The checkout-flow test has a strong engagement signal but no business winner. t1 significantly increases add-to-cart per user, with a consistent direction across every country, though IN remains inconclusive. t2 significantly decreases add-to-cart overall, with clear declines in FR, BR, and DE and no decisive result elsewhere. Neither variant has a conclusive GMV or purchase-per-user effect: each confidence interval crosses zero, leaving room for meaningful gains or harmful losses. Thus, t1’s add-to-cart improvement is not proof of monetization. No sample-ratio mismatch was detected, and the day-level readout reports no underpowered intervals across 56 days per arm. However, 48 tests weaken isolated slice claims, and the weekly ramp was not stable. The defensible verdict remains NO-WINNER: retain t1 as a promising add-to-cart candidate, but require clearer downstream GMV and purchase evidence; reject t2’s overall direction.
